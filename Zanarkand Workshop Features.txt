ZANARKAND WORKSHOP v0.4.0 — FEATURE SUMMARY
============================================

EDITORS
-------
- Monsters, loot, statuses, elements, stats, and Battle Scripts
- Auto Abilities, text, effects, stat bonuses, and recipes
- Items and Player, Aeon, standard enemy, and boss commands
- Rikku Mix recipes
- Battle formations and coordinates
- Original, Standard, and Expert Sphere Grids
- Treasure rewards on reconstructed field maps

SAFETY AND RECOVERY
-------------------
- Range-checked numeric editing with Enter to apply and Escape to cancel
- Structure-aware rebuilding for variable-length text and Battle Scripts
- Recovery from a verified clean master folder
- Single-entry recovery for Auto Abilities and treasure rewards
- Validation before edited files are written

LIVE UTILITIES
--------------
- Inventory, Arena, and Battle trackers
- Debug and live-memory tools when Final Fantasy X is running

COMPATIBILITY
-------------
- Windows Steam release of Final Fantasy X/X-2 HD Remaster
- US data in the new_uspc and jppc master folders
